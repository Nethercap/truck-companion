# Truck Dash Beta for Android

A test version of the native Android app: its own interface instead of the website in a
window. It installs next to the Truck Dash app from Google Play and doesn't replace it, so you
can keep using that one. Expect rough edges, and please tell us about them.

You need Android 7 or newer, and Truck Dash on the PC where you play, the same as for the website.

## Install

1. Download `TruckDash-Beta-<version>-arm64-v8a.apk`, the right one for almost any phone. If
   Android says the app wasn't installed or isn't compatible, use the `armeabi-v7a` one: some
   older phones run 32-bit Android (the Moto G5, for example). The `universal` one works on
   all of them, but it's much bigger.
2. Open the file. Android asks you to allow installing from your browser, and Play Protect may
   warn you about an unknown app, because it doesn't come from Google Play.
3. To update, install the new version over this one. There's no need to uninstall.

## What to try

- Connect with the code from the Truck Dash tray icon on your PC, or over your local network.
- The live map, a route to your job, and the voice guidance.
- Leave the app with the game running: the notification should keep showing the next turn, and
  the voice should keep talking, also with the screen off.
- Link your account, then look at your stats.
- A convoy, the floating window, and the buttons that send keys to the game.

## If something goes wrong

- **The app closed by itself:** the next time you open it, it offers a report. Tap Share and send
  the file to whoever gave you this beta. It has the error, the app's recent log and your phone
  model, not your account or your codes.
- **A wrong turn or a bad route:** Settings > Report a navigation problem.
- **Anything else:** tell us what happened, your phone model and the app version, which is at the
  bottom of Settings.

## Not here yet

- The website's classic and truck gauge styles, and the dashboard around the map. A second device
  can still open them: Settings copies the link.
- Moving the map buttons around, and sharing a single trip or the driven map as an image.
- Maps made with "Build my map" reach the phone only over your local network.
- Translations other than English and Spanish haven't been reviewed by native speakers.
